About

Design at the edge of order.

Tanishq Bajpai is an architect and spatial thinker working across scales — from intimate residences to urban interventions. The work believes architecture should provoke, shelter, and surprise in equal measure.

Drawing from postmodern thinking: history is not rejected but remixed, ornament is not a crime but a language, and contradiction is a design tool.

Based in Prayagraj. Working globally.
